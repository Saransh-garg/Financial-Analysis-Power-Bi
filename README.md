# **Financial Analysis for Credit Card Usage**
## **Project Overview**
As a Financial Data Analyst, you are tasked with analyzing credit card usage and financial metrics for a banking institution. Using Power BI and DAX functions, you will calculate important financial metrics like running totals, moving averages, growth rates, and KPIs. The goal is to assess customer behavior, credit utilization, delinquency risk, and more, providing key insights for improving customer retention and financial performance.

## **Key Metrics and KPIs**
**Running Total of Credit Card Transactions**
- Track cumulative transaction amounts over time.

**4-Week Moving Average of Credit Limit**
- Calculate the rolling average of the credit limit for each client over the past 4 weeks.

**Month-over-Month (MoM) and Week-over-Week (WoW) Growth**
- Assess the growth in transaction amounts over the past month and week.

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**
- Calculate CAC as a ratio of transaction amounts to assess the cost of acquiring customers.

**Yearly Average of Avg Utilization Ratio**
- Calculate the yearly average of the average utilization ratio for all clients.

**Interest Earned Percentage**
- Calculate the percentage of interest earned compared to the total revolving balance for each client.

**Top 5 Clients by Total Transaction Amount**
- Identify the top clients based on total transaction amount.

**High Utilization Clients**
- Flag clients whose average utilization ratio exceeds 80%.

**Customer Churn Indicator**
- Create a KPI that flags clients who have not made any transactions in the last 6 months.

**Delinquency Rate**
- Calculate the percentage of clients with a delinquent account greater than zero.

**Credit Risk**
- Create a risk score for each client based on their utilization ratio, delinquency status, and total revolving balance.

**Income vs Credit Limit Correlation**
- Analyze the correlation between income and credit limit for all clients.

**Customer Satisfaction by Credit Card Category**
- Calculate the average customer satisfaction score by credit card category.

**Loan Approval vs Credit Limit**
- Analyze how the credit limit impacts personal loan approval by comparing credit limits for clients with and without loans.

**High-Risk Clients Flag**
- Flag clients whose total revolving balance exceeds 90% of their credit limit and have a high average utilization ratio.
